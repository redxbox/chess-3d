# Privacy Policy — Chess 3D

**Effective date: 26 September 2026**

Chess 3D is an offline-first chess game published by RedXbox.

## Data collection

Chess 3D does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking technology, or online multiplayer service.

## Local data

Preferences, saved games, PGN history, and optional diagnostic information remain in the app's private storage on the user's device. Android may remove this data when the app is uninstalled. Android cloud backup is disabled for this application.

## Diagnostics and feedback

The current beta does not automatically upload crash reports. If a tester chooses to report a problem, they control what information they send through their chosen communication channel. Diagnostic sharing must always be voluntary and initiated by the user.

## Permissions and network access

The game is designed to work without a network connection. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or photos. Android may expose platform services required to install and run the application.

## Children

Because the application does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published with an updated effective date before a release that introduces relevant data processing.

## Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be submitted through the public issue tracker:
https://github.com/redxbox/chess-3d/issues
