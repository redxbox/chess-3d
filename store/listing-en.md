## Full description
Play a polished game of chess anywhere, completely offline.

Chess 3D combines a classic presentation with touch-first Android controls. Challenge four levels of computer opponent, play a local two-player match, use chess clocks, review move history, and keep a private archive of finished games.

Features:
- Complete chess rules, including castling, en passant, promotion, repetition and draw rules
- Four offline AI difficulty levels
- Local two-player mode
- Optional chess clocks and undo controls
- FEN and PGN support with a private local archive
- Smooth 3D board camera with touch orbit and pinch zoom
- Low, Medium, High and adaptive graphics profiles
- High contrast, colorblind palette, reduced motion and large-text options
- English and Persian interface
- Ambient music and event-specific sound effects
- No account, ads, analytics or required internet connection

Your games and preferences stay on your device.
